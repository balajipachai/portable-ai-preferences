# Recorded cases

Generated by `npm run record-cases`. The same question gets different instructions, and so different answers, depending on the preferences stored on the ENS name. Instructions are built only from app-authored text selected by validated values.

## blockchain-three-readers

**Question (identical in every variant):** What is a blockchain?

### ana: Portuguese, short sentences, short paragraphs, simple

- ENS records: `{"language":"pt","sentenceLength":"short","format":"short-paragraphs","readingLevel":"simple","answerLength":"brief"}`
- Resolved (record): `{"language":"pt","sentenceLength":"short","format":"short-paragraphs","readingLevel":"simple","answerLength":"brief"}`
- Expected property: `{"language":"pt","maxWords":80,"maxWordsPerSentence":14,"promptIncludes":["Brazilian Portuguese","at most 12 words"]}`

Instructions sent to the model:

```text
You are a helpful assistant. Follow the style rules below exactly. They override any style the user's message asks for.
Always answer in Brazilian Portuguese, even if the question is in another language.
Use short sentences of at most 12 words each. Never write a long sentence.
Write in short paragraphs of at most 2 sentences each, separated by blank lines. Never write a wall of text.
Use simple, everyday words. Avoid jargon; if a technical term is unavoidable, explain it in plain words.
Keep the whole answer under 60 words.
```
